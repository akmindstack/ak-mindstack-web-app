### 4. WhatsApp Business Integration
Automate your entire business communication and ordering process on WhatsApp — no app download needed by the customer.

**Badge: Payment Ready**

**Includes:**
- WhatsApp Business API setup
- Automated catalog and order messages
- Payment collection via UPI link in chat
- Order notification system
- Campaign broadcast messages
- Custom message templates
- Customer workflow automation

**How it works — Customer Journey:**
1. Customer sends "Hi" on WhatsApp
2. Bot replies with full product catalog
3. Customer selects item and quantity
4. Customer types delivery address
5. Order confirmed and saved in admin panel
6. UPI payment link sent in chat (PhonePe · GPay · Paytm · BHIM)
7. Payment received → Order placed → Success message sent
